Bozor Match Privacy Policy

Developer: Bozor Match Games
Contact: bestjasurbek@gmail.com
Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the offline Android game Bozor Match (package com.bozormatch.game) handles information.

Information handled on your device

The game saves your level, active puzzle, coins, stars, lives and refill times, helper inventory, market restoration progress, daily gifts, language and settings in the app's local storage. It also keeps a local recovery copy of the save file. These records allow the game to resume and recover interrupted saves.

The game may write gameplay events and technical errors to local diagnostic logs. The current game does not send these logs or your progress to the developer.

Collection and sharing

The current game works offline and does not collect or share personal information with the developer or third parties. It has no account registration, advertising SDK, remote analytics, cloud save or social features. It does not request internet access, contacts, camera, microphone, location or advertising identifier access.

Device permission

The game uses Android's vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Retention and deletion

Progress stays in the app's storage until it is cleared or the game is uninstalled. You can delete the local progress and its local recovery copy through Android Settings → Apps → Bozor Match → Storage → Clear storage. This resets the game. Android or device backup services, if enabled, are controlled by your device and service settings.

Support

If you contact the support email above, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Email delivery is handled by your email provider and ours under their own policies.

Other services

Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own policies. This policy describes the game itself.

Changes

If the game's information practices change, this policy will be updated and any required notices or choices will be provided in the game.
